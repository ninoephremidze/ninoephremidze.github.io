Lensing visualizations

Gravitational waves passing a dark matter subhalo

Gravitational waves from merging black holes have wavelengths of hundreds to thousands of kilometres. When a lens is comparable in size, the waves do not just bend: they diffract and interfere, leaving a frequency-dependent imprint on the signal.

Change the lens mass to move from diffraction, where the lens is almost invisible, through interference, to geometric optics, where the detector records two copies of the signal. Switch the lens profile to see how the way dark matter is distributed changes the imprint.

What you are looking at

The lensed signal is the unlensed one multiplied, frequency by frequency, by the amplification factor F(w, y), with w = 8πG MLz f / c³. Below w ≈ 1 the wavelength is larger than the lens and F ≈ 1. Well above it, F is the sum of two images, the second delayed by Δt, which shows up as regular oscillations in |F(f)| and as beating, then an echo, in the waveform.

F is computed with FIONA, which evaluates the lensing diffraction integral with fast Hankel transforms, for four lens profiles: a point mass (a primordial black hole or compact clump), a cored isothermal sphere, an NFW halo, and the soliton core of ultra-light axion dark matter. Lengths are in units of the Einstein radius of a point mass MLz. The page stores FIONA's wave-optics correction to the geometric-optics image sum on a grid in w and y, and adds the image sum back; above w = 100 it uses geometric optics alone. For the point mass the tables agree with the exact solution (Takahashi & Nakamura 2003) to 3×10⁻³ or better, and interpolating the tables adds less than 10⁻³ for most settings. The waveform is a simple inspiral–merger–ringdown model with the right shape, not a precision template.

The wave field above is a two-dimensional Huygens–Fresnel calculation through a thin gravitational phase screen with the selected profile's lensing potential. It shows the same physics, diffraction and interference behind the lens, but its wavelength and geometry are chosen for display.
